Common questions

Who do you work with?

Teams at every level, schools, businesses, brands, artists, live events, training facilities, churches and community groups, and individual athletes.

Where are you based? Do you travel?

Wes Way is based on Long Island and works across New York City and Long Island regularly. Projects outside New York are welcome too, and travel costs are included in your quote so there are no surprises.

What’s the difference between a single shoot and a monthly retainer?

A single shoot is a one-time booking: one game, one event, or one session, with the edited content delivered after. A retainer is ongoing. You pay a set amount each month for a set number of shoot days and deliverables (for example, two game days, four highlight videos, and a batch of graphics). Because it’s booked together, each shoot costs less than it would on its own.

Which option is right for me?

If you have a single event or want to test things out, start with a single shoot. If you need content every week or month (a season, a tour, a gym, a program), a retainer usually saves money and keeps your pages active. A brand film is best for a bigger, one-time story like a launch, a recruiting video, or an introduction.

How does pricing work?

Every quote is custom. It depends on how many shoot days you need, how much gets delivered, and whether it’s one-time or ongoing. After a short call, you get a clear written quote before anything is booked. Smaller budgets are welcome.

What do I actually receive?

Finished content that’s ready to post: edited videos sized for Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms, plus photo galleries and graphics if they’re part of your package. Retainer clients can also get a content calendar and help posting.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your schedule, access to the games, shows, or sessions, and a quick call about your goals. Everything else is handled from there.
