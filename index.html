Exit 09 FAQ
Who do you work with?

Anyone in sports: brands, pro and amateur teams, leagues, events, training facilities, and individual athletes. If your world revolves around the game, we're a fit.

Where are you based? Do you travel?

We're based in New York City and cover NYC and Long Island regularly. Travel is on the table for the right project.

How does pricing work?

Every quote is built around what you need: how many shoot days, how many teams, and what gets delivered. Retainers are bundled, so they cost less per shoot than booking one at a time. We also build packages for tighter budgets.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your schedule, access to the games or sessions, and a quick call about what you want people to see. We handle the rest.

Do you post the content for us?

We can. Every package comes with files ready to post. Retainer clients can add content calendars, captions, and posting on their accounts.
