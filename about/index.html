Exit 01 The Founder

A storyteller first.

I'm Jamien Petty, a New York producer and Syracuse Newhouse grad. The camera is just how I tell the story.

For the last four years I've told stories at the collegiate and professional level: on the sidelines for Syracuse Athletics, in MLB stadiums with the Players Association, and on the floor at Fanatics Fest. I've seen what a dedicated content team can do, and how hard that is to get without one.

That's why I started Wes Way: to bring that same level of work to teams, artists, and brands without them needing to hire a full-time staff.
