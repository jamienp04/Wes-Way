I’ve been filming for as long as I can remember. Car rides, pickup games, events, whatever was in front of me. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a hobby.

Over the last few years I’ve been on the sidelines for Syracuse Athletics, in MLB stadiums with the Players Association, and on the floor at Fanatics Fest. I saw what a dedicated content team can do, and how hard that is to get without one.

That’s why I started Wes Way: to bring that same level of work to teams, businesses, artists, and brands, without them needing a full-time staff.
